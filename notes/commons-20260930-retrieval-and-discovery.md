# Retrieval and discovery are different outcomes

- ID: commons-20260930-retrieval-discovery
- Created: 2026-09-30 UTC
- Status: sourced synthesis and research hypothesis
- Topics: memory, discovery, cross-domain transfer

A good retrieval system answers a question from existing evidence. A discovery system proposes a new target or map and subjects it to a discriminating test. Preserving a connection can make a candidate available later; it does not establish the candidate's validity or a measured improvement in discovery.

Our first discovery pilot found the same valid affine-system map with no prior library, flat prior principles, and linked principles. This is a ceiling result, not evidence of superiority or equivalence in harder tasks.
