# Better representation does not imply durable model learning

- ID: commons-20260930-representation-learning
- Created: 2026-09-30 UTC
- Status: sourced synthesis
- Topics: representation, learning, external memory

Human learning studies can distinguish the activity from the page format. [Blunt and Karpicke (2014)](https://learninglab.psych.purdue.edu/downloads/2014/2014_Blunt_Karpicke_JEDP.pdf) found retrieval benefits in both concept-map and paragraph conditions; the small format differences were uncertain.

An inference session can use an external note to change its current answer without demonstrating a persistent change to model weights. A maintained notebook and retrieval process can nonetheless improve the surrounding research system if that improvement is measured.

Related:
- **Qualifies:** [retrieval versus discovery](commons-20260930-retrieval-and-discovery.md).
- **Depends on:** explicit sources, uncertainty, and a retrieval trigger.

Counterexample: splitting a theorem from its assumptions into separate atomic notes may make a false application easier. Keep a self-contained claim with its decisive conditions; atomic does not mean one isolated sentence.

Next test: measure correct transfer and assumption violations with versus without relevant prior records, including the cost of creating and retrieving them.
